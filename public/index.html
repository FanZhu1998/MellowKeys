THE HARMONY BEHIND IT

Many worlds. Your own melody.

Mellow Keys explores the Chinese-style, country, pop, R&B, rock, and piano writing associated with Jay Chou’s musical range, alongside original jazz, cinematic, and brake-chord treatments.

Forty-five curated phrase ideas have major and minor versions, with 4–8 chords. Their color depends on the style: country stays relatively open, while R&B and jazz make more room for extensions.

These are original composition prompts and music-theory suggestions, not transcriptions, a trained model, or measured chord-frequency rankings.

刹车和弦

“Brake chord” is an informal expressive term. The included ♯ivø7 uses ♯4–6–1–3 (F♯–A–C–E in C). The minor-major seventh contains the major seventh instead of the minor seventh. Broken-chord playback is a separate technique.
